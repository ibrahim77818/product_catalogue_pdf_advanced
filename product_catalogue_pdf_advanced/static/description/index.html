Why this module

Sales teams need a catalogue they can hand to a customer. Odoo has the products, the prices and the stock; this module turns them into a document.

In standard Odoo 17

  • The Pricelist report is a table of prices: no images, no descriptions, no design choices.
  • There is no catalogue to configure: no cover, no table of contents, no columns, no category pages.
  • Long HTML reports let the PDF engine choose where pages break, so blocks can be cut between sheets.
  • Emailing one PDF to several customers sends them the same prices, whatever their own pricelist.
  • Printed price lists say nothing about availability, or you have to expose exact quantities.
  • Long product ranges make the PDF engine use a lot of memory and can fail on large catalogues.

With this module

  • Product cards with images, reference, description, price, availability badge and QR code.
  • A Product Catalogue record per audience: cover page, table of contents, 1–4 boxes per row, accent colour.
  • Pages are planned in millimetres before printing; each card has a fixed size and never splits.
  • Send by Email groups recipients by pricelist and language and renders one PDF per group.
  • In stock / Low stock / On request badges for one warehouse, never the quantity itself.
  • Rendering in batches of 40 pages, merged into one PDF, with a cache that reuses recent PDFs.

How it works

Four steps from product data to a customer’s inbox.

1
Select
Pick products by category, tag, attribute value or an explicit list, and choose a pricelist and a warehouse.
2
Design
Choose a style, boxes per row, image size, paper, accent colour and what each card shows.
3
Print
The module plans every page, prices each variant with Odoo’s pricelist engine and renders the PDF.
4
Send
Each customer receives the catalogue with their own pricelist, in their own language, logged in the chatter.

Features

Screenshots use the module’s demo data: 25 office-furniture products in 5 categories, a public and a trade pricelist (−12%), three customers and four ready-made catalogues.

Menus

A Catalogues app with one record per audience

Open Catalogues › Catalogues. Each catalogue shows its type, style, pricelist, language and the number of products it currently selects. Email templates are under Catalogues › Configuration › Email Templates.

Feature 1

Choose exactly which products go in

On the Products tab, combine Categories (sub-categories included), Product Tags, Attribute Values, Only These Products and Excluded Products. Can be Sold Only is on by default, and an Advanced Filter domain is available in developer mode. The Products smart button opens the current selection, and Max Products protects against a catalogue that grows too large.

Feature 2

Design: style, columns, paper and page breaks

On the Design tab pick a Style (Grid cards, Compact list or Showcase), Boxes per Row, Image Size and Print Quality, the Paper Size and Orientation, and an Accent Color. Add a Cover Page with title, subtitle and image, a Table of Contents, Group By Category, a New Page per Category and a Max Rows Per Page.

Feature 3

Show or hide each piece of information

On the Content tab, switch on or off Show Image, Show Internal Reference, Show Description (sales or product description), Show Price, Show Price Before Discount, Show Product Link (QR) and Show Availability. Prices are Tax excluded or Tax included. Availability uses one Warehouse and a Low Stock Threshold, and Hide Unavailable Products drops what you cannot deliver.

QR links: set a Product URL Pattern such as https://shop.example.com/p/{default_code} (placeholders {base_url}, {id}, {default_code}, {barcode}). When Website Sale is installed and no pattern is set, the product’s eCommerce page is used. A product whose placeholder is empty gets no QR code rather than a broken link.
Feature 4

A finished document: cover, contents and product pages

Click Print PDF. The cover lists the products, edition date, pricelist and tax mode, validity and the categories inside. The Contents page points at real page numbers. Product pages carry a running header with the current category, a category band (repeated as continued when a category spills over) and a footer that states the currency, tax mode, pricelist, warehouse, generation time and page n / N.

Feature 5

Prices from your pricelists, availability without quantities

Each variant is priced by Odoo’s own pricelist engine. Products whose variants have different prices print From the lowest one. On a pricelist that shows its discount, the public price is struck through above the customer price. The badge reads In stock, Low stock or On request; the quantity itself is never printed.

Feature 6

Arabic and right-to-left catalogues

Set the catalogue’s Language to Arabic, or let each recipient receive their own language. The whole layout mirrors right-to-left, product names and descriptions are printed in the chosen language, and every printed label is translated. The module embeds IBM Plex Sans Arabic, which covers Latin and Arabic, so the result does not depend on fonts installed on the server.

Feature 7

Compact price lists and landscape lookbooks

Compact list prints one dense line per product, ideal for long ranges and internal price lists. Showcase gives each product a large image and its full description, one or two per row, here in landscape for a lookbook of the products tagged New. A pale accent colour is darkened automatically so printed prices stay readable.

Feature 8

Send by email without leaking prices

Click Send by Email and add recipients. With Use Each Recipient’s Pricelist and Use Each Recipient’s Language ticked, the wizard shows how recipients will be grouped before you send. Each group receives one email with its own PDF, built from the Product Catalogue: Send by email template, and the sending is logged in the chatter.

The chatter keeps a record of which pricelist and language each recipient received.

Print-accurate by design

Odoo 17 prints with wkhtmltopdf, an engine that supports neither modern CSS grids nor reliable page breaks. So the module does not leave page breaks to it.

Measure Each style knows the exact height, in millimetres, of a row of cards for your columns, image size and toggles.
Fit Names and descriptions are shortened with an ellipsis to the lines their box can hold.
Plan Headings and rows are packed into pages; a heading always stays with its first row.
Draw Each page is a fixed-size box, so the PDF has exactly the planned pages.
Checked on real output: on a 1,200-product test catalogue the PDF had exactly the 152 planned pages, every one of the 1,200 product references appeared on a single page, and the QR codes read back from the printed pages decoded to the right product URLs.

Built for real-world corner cases

The situations that break most catalogue reports are handled in code and covered by tests.

Very long product names
Shortened on a word boundary to the lines the card holds, so cards keep their size.
Products without an image
A tinted placeholder with the product’s initial keeps the grid even.
Variants with surcharges
Every variant is priced, and the card prints From the lowest price.
Services and consumables
No availability badge for products whose stock is not tracked.
A category longer than a page
Its band is repeated as continued on the next page; the table of contents points at its first page.
Large catalogues
Products are read in batches of 200, images at the size they print, and the PDF is rendered 40 pages at a time.
Resending the same catalogue
A PDF is reused while configuration, products and pricelist rules are unchanged, within Reuse Generated PDF For (min).
Pale brand colours
The accent is darkened until printed prices reach WCAG AA contrast; the cover switches to dark text.
Multi-company
Catalogues, pricelists, warehouses and products are limited to the catalogue’s company.
